NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2026, by and between Alpha Solutions Inc. ("Disclosing Party"), a Delaware corporation, and Beta Technologies LLC ("Receiving Party"), a California limited liability company.

1. Purpose
The Disclosing Party agrees to share proprietary information with the Receiving Party solely for evaluating a potential joint venture and business partnership between the entities.

2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" encompasses all proprietary data, trade secrets, software architecture, technical specifications, business plans, customer lists, and financial forecasts disclosed in oral, visual, or written form.

3. Obligations of Receiving Party
The Receiving Party agrees to maintain the strict confidentiality of all Confidential Information, using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential assets, and no less than reasonable care. Receiving Party shall not disclose, replicate, distribute, or reverse-engineer any proprietary materials.

4. Term and Termination
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the date of execution. Obligations of non-disclosure regarding trade secrets survive indefinitely.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement by their authorized representatives.
